Squeezed icons

Built on top of the unified set. The wrapper <g transform="…"> is baked into each path's coordinates, then everything is rounded to integer precision in the new 0 – 1000 space. Consecutive same-letter commands are merged. Whitespace between letters and digits is dropped. No fidelity loss at icon sizes — 1 unit at 300 px = 0.3 px, well below pixel resolution.

01
Bake the unified transform
Apply each icon's wrapper translate(tx ty) scale(s) directly to every path coordinate. Drop the wrapper <g> — one less DOM node per icon.
≈ 60 B / file
02
Integer precision
Round every coordinate to the nearest integer in the new 0–1000 space. Removes the decimal point AND the digit after it from thousands of numbers.
≈ 30 % saved
03
Merge consecutive commands
L 5 6 L 7 8 L 9 10 → L5 6 7 8 9 10. Same for C, Q, S, T, A. Implicit L after M.
≈ 5 % saved
04
Tight whitespace
No space between a letter and the digit that follows. Negative signs absorb the separator: 5 -3 → 5-3.
≈ 3 % saved
